Nothing Works

The tool finds your League installations automatically, so start the League client and give it a few seconds. The banner at the top shows which one it is using.

If it points at the wrong installation, or your install was not found:
1. Make sure the League client is running
2. Use the banner at the top of the tool
3. Click "Switch to Live" or "Switch to PBE" to change installation
4. Or click "Browse..." and pick the folder that contains LeagueClient.exe

Your choice is remembered, so you only have to do this once per installation.

Can I use this with PBE?

Yes. Live and PBE are detected separately. When both are installed the banner offers a button to switch between them, and the tool only talks to the one you select.

My icon resets after every game

League re-checks your icon when you get into a game or the client refreshes, and unowned icons applied through this tool can be reset by that. Enable "Auto re-apply icon" in the Custom Icons tab and the tool will watch for resets and put your last applied icon back automatically.

What are the CN icons?

Icons 50 to 78 are chibi champion icons that were only ever released on the Chinese server, but they still exist in the game data everywhere. The Custom Icons tab lists them with a CN badge. Riot rejects them as account icons because they are not owned, so they apply to your social/profile-card presence only, and other players in your region may not see them.
